Skills into opportunity

Build a career around better work.

Workspace sits at the intersection of people, process, data, and technology. That creates several routes into IT.

01 / Career opportunities

Three roles to explore.

Systems and people

Google Workspace Administrator

Configures users, groups, devices, sharing, security, and service settings. This role suits someone who enjoys solving problems and creating reliable systems for an organization.

Change and adoption

Digital Adoption Specialist

Helps teams move to new tools through training, documentation, communication, and support. Success depends on empathy, clear writing, and an understanding of workplace habits.

Automation and insight

Collaboration Solutions Consultant

Designs Workspace-based workflows for clients, often connecting Sheets, Forms, Apps Script, and third-party systems. This path combines technical thinking with business analysis.

02 / Recognised pathways

Learn, practise, prove.

Google Workspace Administrator Professional Certificate

Google Cloud training pathway covering administration, security, troubleshooting, and practical scenarios. It is a useful structured starting point for an administrator portfolio.

Google Workspace Administrator Help and training

Official documentation and learning materials support hands-on practice with organizations, users, groups, Gmail, Drive, and security settings.

Google Cloud Certification: Associate Cloud Engineer

A broader recognised certification for cloud foundations and operations. It complements Workspace knowledge for people who want to move toward cloud support or engineering.

CompTIA A+ and IT support learning

A widely recognised entry-level IT pathway covering troubleshooting, operating systems, networking, and security. These foundations make Workspace support more effective.

03 / A practical starting point

Make evidence, not just claims.

Create a small portfolio: document a mock Workspace setup, write a user onboarding guide, build a Sheets dashboard, and record the security decisions you made. Pair this evidence with official training and customer-service skills.
