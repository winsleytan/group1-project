Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Is Google Workspace free to use?

Gmail, Docs, Drive, Meet, and Calendar are free for personal use with a Google account. Schools and organizations can upgrade to paid plans for extra storage, admin controls, and security features.

How much does Google Workspace cost?

Google Workspace business plans start at $7 per user per month with a one-year commitment. Prices and plan features may vary by region. (Click to view plans and purchase Google Workspace).

Can I use Workspace apps offline?

Yes. Docs, Sheets, and Slides support offline editing once enabled in your browser or the mobile app, and changes sync automatically when you're back online.

How many people can join a Google Meet call?

Free accounts support up to 100 participants per call, with longer call limits and larger meeting sizes available on paid plans.

Is my data private and secure?

Google Workspace encrypts data in transit and at rest, and gives you full control over sharing permissions for every file and folder.
