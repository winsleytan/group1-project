Cybersecurity Careers

Cybersecurity offers different career opportunities for people who are interested in technology, problem-solving and protecting digital information.

Different cybersecurity roles require different skills and areas of knowledge. Education, practical experience and professional certifications can help individuals develop their skills.

Career Opportunities

Cybersecurity Analyst

Monitors systems and helps identify cybersecurity risks and suspicious activities.

Useful skills include security awareness, networking and problem-solving.

Security Engineer

Helps design, implement and maintain security systems and controls within an organisation.

Useful skills include networking, system security and technical problem-solving.

Security Consultant

Provides advice and support to organisations on cybersecurity risks and security practices.

Useful skills include communication, analysis and cybersecurity knowledge.

Certifications and Learning Pathways

Professional certifications can help learners develop knowledge in specific areas of cybersecurity. The appropriate certification may depend on a person's experience, interests and career goals.

Certification / Pathway Focus Area Suitable For
CompTIA Security+ Cybersecurity fundamentals Beginners and early-career learners
Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) Ethical hacking and security testing Learners interested in security testing
Cisco Cybersecurity Networking and cybersecurity Learners developing networking and security skills
Microsoft Security Learning Security technologies and cloud Learners interested in Microsoft technologies

Skills for Cybersecurity Careers

Technical knowledge is important, but cybersecurity professionals also need workplace and problem-solving skills.

  • Problem-solving
  • Analytical thinking
  • Communication
  • Attention to detail
  • Technical knowledge
  • Continuous learning

Building a Career in Cybersecurity

A cybersecurity career can begin with learning basic technology and security concepts. As knowledge and experience develop, learners can explore specialised areas and professional certifications that match their interests.
