My Cybersecurity Learning Journey

Cybersecurity is a field that continues to change as new technologies and digital threats develop. Learning about cybersecurity is therefore not something that ends after completing a course or programme.

As a student, I can develop my cybersecurity knowledge by learning about common threats, practising safe digital habits and exploring new cybersecurity technologies.

Why is Lifelong Learning Important?

Technology changes over time, and cybersecurity practices also need to change to address new risks. Continuous learning can help individuals keep their knowledge and skills relevant.

  • Keep up with changes in technology
  • Understand new cybersecurity threats
  • Develop useful digital skills
  • Improve awareness of online risks
  • Prepare for future career opportunities

Skills I Want to Develop

There are several cybersecurity-related skills that I would like to improve as I continue learning.

Cybersecurity Awareness

Understand common threats such as phishing, malware and social engineering.

Network Security

Learn more about how networks are protected from unauthorised access.

Digital Safety

Improve safe practices when using online accounts, devices and digital services.

Lifelong Learning Resources

The following resources can be used to continue learning about cybersecurity and digital skills.

  1. Coursera – Provides online courses covering cybersecurity and other technology-related subjects. It is useful because I can learn at my own pace. It can support my future learning by helping me develop more cybersecurity knowledge and skills.
  2. edX – Offers online courses and programmes from universities and educational institutions. It is useful because I can learn from structured courses and study at my own pace. It can support my future learning by helping me build more knowledge about cybersecurity.
  3. Cisco Networking Academy – Provides learning resources related to networking, cybersecurity and digital skills. It is useful because I can develop practical knowledge about cybersecurity and networking. It can support my future learning by helping me build technical skills for further study.
  4. Microsoft Learn – Provides self-paced learning resources for Microsoft technologies and digital skills. It is useful because I can learn technical topics at my own pace. It can support my future learning by helping me develop digital and cybersecurity-related skills.
  5. IBM SkillsBuild – Provides free learning resources covering technology and workplace skills. It is useful because I can access learning materials without cost and explore different technology topics. It can support my future learning by helping me continue developing digital and cybersecurity skills.

My Reflection

Learning about cybersecurity has helped me understand that technology is useful but also comes with risks. I have learned that simple actions, such as using strong passwords and being careful with links, can contribute to safer digital behaviour.

In the future, I would like to continue developing my cybersecurity knowledge and explore how these skills can be applied in education, work and everyday life.
