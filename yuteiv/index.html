Welcome to CyberShield

Cybersecurity is an important part of modern digital workplaces. It helps protect computers, networks, systems and information from unauthorised access and digital threats.

As people and organisations increasingly depend on digital technology, understanding cybersecurity has become an important digital skill for students, employees and future professionals.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to the protection of digital systems, networks and information from threats. It involves practices and technologies that help organisations keep their information and digital resources secure.

Protect Information

Cybersecurity helps protect important personal, business and organisational information.

Secure Systems

Security measures help protect computers, networks and other digital systems.

Reduce Risks

Cybersecurity awareness can help people recognise and reduce common digital security risks.
